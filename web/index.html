Controls & saving

Enter or tap readies your recipe. Hold Space or the main button to pour. In Easy, tap the same button to serve, or hold it to top up. Enter opens a ready review or serves in Easy. P or Escape pauses. Leaving or pausing during a Hardcore pour ends that attempt.

Your finished cup waits for you. Patience starts with your first recipe change; complex orders allow more time. Menus and settling pause it. Ingredients lock at the first pour. Changing difficulty starts a new streak.

Music and effects have separate controls. Your business saves on this device; export a backup from Build. Imported earlier saves keep their career progress.
