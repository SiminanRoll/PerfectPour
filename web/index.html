How to play
◒

Match their recipe.Choose the base and add-ins shown on the order. Tap a jar to cycle its quantity.

①②

Mix two sodas.Follow the numbered bottles and fill lines. Hardcore gives one hold per soda; the last release serves.

≋

Leave room for fizz.Foam settles into soda. The meter shows both; aim for the final liquid level.

★

Earn your stars.Match the recipe and fill. Good reviews grow streaks and future prices.

⌂

Grow your bar.Buy equipment, ingredients, seats, and staff. Receipts show what you earn and spend.

Controls & saving

Enter or tap readies your recipe. Hold Space or the main button to pour. In Easy, tap or press Enter to switch soda or serve; hold to top up. Enter opens a ready review. P or Escape pauses. Leaving or pausing during a Hardcore pour ends that attempt.

Your finished cup waits for you. Patience starts with your first recipe change; complex orders allow more time. Menus and settling pause it. Ingredients lock at the first pour. Changing difficulty starts a new streak.

Music and effects have separate controls. Your business saves on this device; export a backup from Build. Imported earlier saves keep their career progress.
