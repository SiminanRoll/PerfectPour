A QUICK REFRESHER

The art of the pour.

Hardcore is the default. Hold Space or the pour button once. Release to automatically serve. No second attempt and no topping up. This mode earns 15% extra base pay.

Easy gives you time. Release to stop, wait for the foam to settle, and top up. Use Enter or Serve Drink when ready. Choose a mode before starting a cup; changing it resets your streak.

Liquid is what counts. The dotted line marks the requested liquid volume. The side bar stacks liquid and foam, just like the cup. Foam can overflow before the soda reaches the rim. When served, foam settles; the beverage inside it counts, but the gas does not.

Build a streak. Four- and five-star pours extend your combo. Milestones at 3, 5, and 8 good pours add cash; five-star pours also earn a perfect-pour tip. A lower review breaks the streak.

Build a business. Visit Your soda bar to buy upgrades and hire employees. Seating and new hires start next day. Wages are reserved while a shift is open and paid once at closing. Better reviews improve your next customer’s price.

P or Escape pauses. If you pause or leave the window while pouring in Hardcore, your one pour ends and is served. Progress saves after each customer and purchase. Export a save to keep a portable backup.

A brand-new business?
